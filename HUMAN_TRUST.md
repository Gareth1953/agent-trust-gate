# Agent Trust Gate™ — Human Trust Architecture

Agent Trust Gate™ is not only about whether machines can verify other machines.

Consequential AI-agent systems ultimately affect people: customers, clients, employees, account holders, borrowers, claimants, suppliers and other humans who may not understand AI, cryptography or agent governance.

ATG therefore treats technical trust as incomplete until it can be connected to human-understandable evidence, organisational accountability and a clear human recourse path.

> **AI does not exist for AI. It serves people.**

> **Trust for the machine. Accountability for the organisation. Proof for the human. Recourse when something goes wrong.**

## Human trust architecture

```text
Human Intent / Need
→ Organisational Authority
→ Human Duty Profile
→ Agent Standing
→ Machine Verification
→ Exact-Action Control
→ Verified Human Authority where required
→ GatePass or Refusal
→ Execution Evidence
→ Human Trust Receipt
→ Accountability and Recourse
```

The human-facing model has three core additions to ATG's existing proof system:

### 1. Human Duty Profile

The task itself is not the whole mandate.

Where an organisation deploys an agent toward a person, the organisation's relevant duties, policy constraints, disclosures, human-review requirements, limits, escalation triggers and recourse route should be translated into machine-checkable constraints before a consequential action is allowed.

> **The task is not the whole mandate. The human relationship is part of the mandate.**

Read: [Human Duty Profile — Agent-to-Human Responsibility Constraints](docs/human-duty-profile.md)

### 2. Human Trust Receipt

A non-technical person should be able to see, save, print or retain a simple record of:

- what happened;
- who authorised it;
- what the agent was allowed to do;
- what it was not allowed to change;
- whether required human approval occurred;
- whether execution matched the authorised action;
- which organisation remains accountable;
- what recourse route applies if something went wrong.

Read: [Human Trust Receipt — Human-Visible Proof for AI Actions](docs/human-trust-receipt.md)

### 3. Accountability and Recourse

ATG should not make an AI look trustworthy through confidence, anthropomorphism or persuasive explanation.

The aim is **calibrated trust**: make authority, limits, relevant evidence, accountability and recourse visible so the affected human can understand the basis for reliance.

Read: [Human Trust, Accountability and Recourse for Consequential AI-Agent Actions](docs/human-trust-accountability-and-recourse.md)

## Agent responsibility to humans

ATG does not claim that an AI agent is an independent legal person or that it absorbs the organisation's liability.

Instead, ATG's future **operational agent accountability** direction is to make it possible to prove:

- which agent acted;
- under whose authority it acted;
- which human-facing duty constraints applied;
- what discretion the agent was given;
- what it was prohibited from doing;
- whether required human involvement occurred;
- whether the proposed exact action satisfied those constraints;
- whether execution matched the authorised action;
- which organisation and recourse route remained attached to the outcome.

When consequential human meaning is ambiguous, the safer principle is:

> **Do not infer beyond the evidence. Reduce autonomy or escalate to a human.**

## Money and customer-impacting actions

This architecture is particularly relevant where an agent may affect:

- payments and transfers;
- purchases and checkout;
- refunds, repayments and reversals;
- collections and arrears;
- account changes;
- credit or lending workflows;
- insurance or claims;
- subscriptions and cancellations;
- fraud or account restrictions;
- other customer or client outcomes with financial consequences.

A valid GatePass proves scoped authority conditions. It does **not** prove that every underlying AI judgment is correct, that an agent is universally safe, or that the organisation has satisfied every legal or regulatory requirement.

## Current boundary

The public Agent Trust Gate repository remains a local, synthetic, non-production demonstrator.

The Human Duty Profile and wider human-trust architecture are roadmap and design layers unless a capability is separately implemented, tested and documented. ATG does not currently provide production legal-policy interpretation, live customer decisioning, payment execution, regulatory certification or independent legal-liability determination.

## Guiding principle

> **Do not ask people to trust the AI. Give them enough evidence to understand what it was allowed to do, what duties constrained it, who remains accountable, and what they can do if something goes wrong.**
