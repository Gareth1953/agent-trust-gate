10. Limitations

  • All identities, roles, evidence, supplier labels and four-digit account endings are synthetic fixtures.
  • No real identity, supplier, bank-account, ERP, payment, customer or employee data is used.
  • No bank detail is verified and no fraud, legality, compliance, payment validity or general agent safety conclusion is made.
  • Fixture cryptography can support integrity, binding and signer-related evidence only within the configured local model.
  • A GatePass decision is not proof of execution. A simulated receipt remains separate and proves no external ERP event.
