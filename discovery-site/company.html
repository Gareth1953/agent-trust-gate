About Agent Trust Gate

Building trust at the point AI becomes action.

Agent Trust Gate™ is an independent UK technology project focused on a narrow but increasingly important question: how can an organisation verify authority for one consequential AI-agent action before it happens?

Our focus

Close to the action. Close to the evidence.

ATG does not try to become an entire AI governance platform. It concentrates on the pre-action boundary where a system must decide whether this requester, under this authority, may perform this exact consequential action now.

That focus has shaped the public demonstrator around Agent Standing, Verified Human Authority, exact-action GatePass, refusal behaviour and separate decision/execution evidence.

Mission

Make digital trust verifiable before action, observable during execution and accountable afterwards.

Control before action. Evidence during execution. Accountability after the event.

Operating principles

The way we build matters.

The public project deliberately favours clear boundaries and inspectable evidence over inflated capability claims.

01

Verify before consequence

Trust checks belong before a sensitive action, not only in an audit after the damage is done.

02

Fail closed when proof is unresolved

Unverifiable standing, stale proof, changed action or missing authority should not silently degrade into permission.

03

Keep humans accountable

Where policy requires human authority, the human must be identifiable, current and authorised for the exact action.

04

Make evidence inspectable

Technical reviewers should be able to reproduce core demo behaviour and see refusal paths in public.

05

Separate decision from execution

An allowed action is not automatically an executed action. Different receipts should record different facts.

06

Stay honest about boundaries

Local demonstrator, production system, certification and commercial deployment are different stages and must not be conflated.

Future-ready without future theatre

Designed not to be trapped by today’s assumptions.

ATG’s roadmap considers stronger agent identity, machine-to-machine trust, crypto agility, post-quantum readiness and low-latency risk-tiered gating — while keeping today’s public implementation claims firmly tied to what reviewers can inspect now.

Human-readable trust proof

Technical receipts should ultimately be understandable to customers, operators and accountable decision-makers.

Crypto agility

Proof formats should be able to evolve as algorithms, credentials and evidence standards change.

Fast reaction trust

Low-risk actions may eventually need safe fast paths, while higher-risk actions remain gated or refused.

Serious review welcome

Challenge the architecture. Bring a bounded scenario.

We welcome technical criticism, reviewer feedback and design-partner conversations that respect the public project’s safety boundaries.
