Website information

Privacy.

A deliberately simple public website with no contact forms, advertising trackers or customer-account system.

Website design

Minimal data collection by design.

This public Agent Trust Gate website is intended to provide corporate, technical and reviewer information. It does not contain a user account system, payment checkout, embedded advertising network, newsletter signup form or customer-data upload form.

The site itself does not intentionally set marketing cookies or use advertising pixels. It is designed as a static public website.

Hosting and infrastructure logs

As with ordinary web hosting, the hosting provider and internet infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, requested page, timestamps and security/logging data required to deliver and protect the service. Such processing is controlled by the relevant infrastructure provider’s own terms and privacy practices.

External links

The site links to GitHub and other public technical resources. When you follow an external link, the destination service operates under its own privacy terms.

Email enquiries

Contact by email

If you contact Agent Trust Gate at gareth@agenttrustgate.com, the information you choose to send will be processed for the purpose of reading, responding to and managing that enquiry.

Do not send sensitive material in an initial enquiry.Please avoid secrets, credentials, payment information, banking information, customer records, regulated personal data, private datasets, production logs or confidential source code unless a separate secure and agreed process has first been established.

Your request

If you want an email conversation corrected, stopped or deleted where reasonably applicable, contact the same corporate address and state your request.
