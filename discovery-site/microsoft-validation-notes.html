Evidence boundary

Genuine identity evidence belongs to the controlled companion; older buyer archives still use mock identity. No new Microsoft capability is added to the installable local.7 plugin. The operational record-service candidate remains a separate product path. A private same-host pseudonymous report is not later independent proof of a reviewer ID; no live Microsoft signing token is redistributed.

The GatePass uses public synthetic fixture keys. Its action/receipt clock is fixed to September 2026 while the successful controlled run occurred in October 2026. Receipts therefore cannot establish wall-clock ordering or production authority. No Microsoft purchase approval, MFA assurance, endorsement, certification, bank integration, financial permission or production readiness is claimed.

Bounded privacy scanning supplements human review and cannot prove universal absence of secrets. Raw private evidence, personal-path history, transcripts, browser captures and authentication launchers are excluded from this proposal. Unencrypted records and same-user/admin access remain known limits. Sanitised summary hashes are local integrity checks, not publisher authentication.
