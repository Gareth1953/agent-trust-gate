Fixed safety boundary

Evaluation does not become execution authority.

  • Only atg.evaluate_action is exposed over local MCP stdio.
  • Shadow Mode issues no GatePass and grants no authority.
  • Customer Trust Receipts are non-authorising and cannot act as GatePasses.
  • No live procurement, payment, settlement or customer communication occurs.
  • No production credentials, secrets or unnecessary personal data are accepted.
  • No automatic retry follows an uncertain external-effect state.
