# Controlled Enterprise Review Plan

**Status:** LOCAL REVIEW PROCESS ONLY — NO REVIEWER SELECTED, CONTACTED OR RECORDED.
**Mission authority:** Gareth Price

## Objective

Test whether five suitably qualified human reviewers can understand the Agent Trust Gate™ (`ATG`) supplier bank-detail-change proposition after one controlled, approximately 15–20 minute review. The review asks whether they can identify:

1. what ATG is;
2. the expensive business problem;
3. the exact-action control gap;
4. what ATG tests;
5. what ATG does not claim;
6. the likely buyer;
7. what the proposed paid assessment includes;
8. whether existing controls may already be sufficient;
9. whether ATG could add material value; and
10. whether a paid next step is credible.

The review must establish comprehension, control relevance, overlap with existing tools and the credibility of a bounded paid assessment. It does not test production performance or claim commercial validation.

The proposition is: **Enterprise AI Action Trust and Governance — initially delivered as a local exact-action evaluation.** The headline is: **“Verify authority before a consequential AI-agent action.”** The selected workflow is a supplier bank-detail change before an ERP or payment-master-data update.

## Five required reviewer perspectives

Each result must be supplied by a different human. One person cannot fill two positions.

1. CISO, AI-security or cybersecurity leader.
2. Finance-controls, accounts-payable or procurement-transformation leader.
3. IAM, IGA, PAM or enterprise-architecture specialist.
4. AI-governance, operational-risk or responsible-AI specialist.
5. Enterprise developer, systems integrator or agent-platform specialist.

## Qualification criteria

A result is eligible only when the reviewer:

- is a human with relevant professional experience or clear technical responsibility;
- fits exactly one of the five required perspectives;
- is not the same person as any other counted reviewer;
- completes the controlled materials and questionnaire;
- gives substantive answers in their own words, including objections or an opinion that existing controls are sufficient; and
- has not been offered an endorsement script or conditioned incentive.

Possible future routes are limited to human-approved, permission-respecting routes: existing business contacts who have engaged with ATG, existing innovation-team or security-vendor correspondence, existing public GitHub technical-review routes, warm professional contacts or permission-based public technical-review communities. A route does not establish qualification or willingness.

## Disqualification criteria

An AI-agent response, automated acknowledgement, generic acknowledgement, administrative support ticket, duplicate person, incomplete questionnaire, unverifiable professional relevance, website visit, social impression, GitHub star or generic encouragement is ineligible. A person is also disqualified if a material conflict makes an independent review unreliable or if the response cannot be retained without prohibited personal, confidential or real supplier information.

## Materials to provide

Only the exact assets listed in [Reviewer Materials Index](reviewer-materials-index.md) may be proposed for controlled sharing. All remain local unless Gareth separately approves each asset and route.

## Review sequence

1. Gareth reviews the candidate’s relevance, conflict and route, then decides whether that candidate may occupy one perspective.
2. Gareth reviews and expressly approves the exact invitation. Approval does not itself send it.
3. A separately authorised future mission may send one approved invitation to one approved human through one approved route.
4. The reviewer sees the executive brief and demonstrator, then the valid and refusal examples.
5. The reviewer answers the questionnaire before corrective prompting. Praise is neither requested nor scored.
6. The response is minimised, checked for eligibility and recorded without unnecessary personal data.
7. The local scoring rules classify the result as `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, `FAIL` or `INELIGIBLE`.
8. Only after five distinct eligible perspectives are complete may the overall rule be evaluated.

This mission performs none of steps 1–8 with a real reviewer. It only prepares the process.

## Pass and fail rules

The controlled review passes only when all conditions hold:

- five relevant, distinct human reviewers complete the review, one per perspective;
- at least four of five correctly explain what ATG does;
- at least four of five correctly explain what ATG does not do;
- at least four of five identify the likely buyer and the next commercial step;
- feedback reveals no material misleading claim; and
- at least three identify a plausible control gap, buyer problem or paid-assessment route.

Praise alone is not a pass. Three qualifying results out of five do not pass. Existing controls being sufficient is a valid and useful conclusion. A material claims concern blocks publication even if numerical thresholds are otherwise met.

## Privacy and data minimisation

Collect only a pseudonymous reviewer reference, role category, broad organisation type and feedback required for the decision. Do not request or retain credentials, bank details, supplier records, customer data, confidential architecture, private correspondence or unnecessary personal information. A reviewer may remain unnamed in internal notes. Apply the controls in [Reviewer Feedback Handling](reviewer-feedback-handling.md).

## Stop conditions

Stop the review process and return to Gareth if any proposed action would:

- contact an unapproved person or use an unapproved message or route;
- disclose an unapproved local asset or personal, confidential or real supplier information;
- imply production readiness, fraud prevention, certification, independently established legal authority, customer adoption or reviewer endorsement;
- create an order, payment, commercial commitment, form, contact database, automated follow-up or public post;
- reveal a material misleading claim; or
- require production, identity, ERP, bank, payment, network or external-system access.

## Publication gate

This pack cannot authorise publication. Before any controlled sharing or bounded publication, Gareth must approve the exact asset, audience, route, wording, privacy treatment and result claim. All repository tests and both enterprise validators must pass, pricing must remain labelled **ESTIMATED AND NOT YET MARKET-VALIDATED.**, and no review may be represented as an endorsement. The existing [Enterprise Positioning Publication Gate](enterprise-positioning-publication-gate.md) remains controlling.

## Commercial-validation boundary

The proposed next step is **“Request a scoped workflow-governance assessment.”** The **ATG Consequential-Action Workflow Governance Assessment** remains an estimated 2–4 week offer at **£5,000–£10,000 plus VAT where applicable**. **ESTIMATED AND NOT YET MARKET-VALIDATED.** Reviewer comprehension, interest or praise does not prove demand, validate pricing, create a customer or establish a paid assessment.

The current valid state is: local, synthetic, deterministic, non-production and unconnected. No identity, ERP, bank or payment system is connected. No external action occurs.
