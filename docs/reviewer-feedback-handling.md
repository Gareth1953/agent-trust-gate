# Reviewer Feedback Handling

**Status:** Local handling rules only. No feedback or personal information has been collected.

## Minimum record

If Gareth separately authorises a real review, collect only:

- a pseudonymous reviewer reference;
- one reviewer role category;
- a broad organisation type; and
- the feedback required to determine comprehension, control value, overlap, objections and commercial signal.

The reviewer may remain unnamed in internal notes. Do not collect unnecessary personal information. Do not request or record credentials, bank details, supplier records, customer data, confidential architecture, private correspondence or production configuration.

## Permissions and use

- Gareth approves every external message, exact recipient, route and attachment before it is sent.
- No public quotation is used without the reviewer’s explicit written permission for the exact wording and context.
- No customer or employer logo is used.
- No response is described as endorsement, customer adoption, buying intent or price validation.
- `permissionToQuote: false` is the default.
- A reviewer’s permission to participate is separate from permission to quote.

## Contact controls

No automated follow-up, bulk message, contact database, CRM workflow, data-enrichment step or recurring reminder is permitted. Do not record a call without explicit permission from every participant. An AI response, automated acknowledgement or administrative ticket is not human reviewer feedback.

## Retention and deletion decision

Retain feedback only for as long as necessary to make and evidence the controlled validation decision. Before real collection, Gareth must approve a retention period, storage location, access scope and deletion method. If a response contains prohibited or unnecessary information, stop processing, isolate it from the score record and seek Gareth’s direction; do not copy it into templates.

## Accuracy and reporting

Record objections and “existing controls are sufficient” conclusions faithfully. Do not convert praise into a pass, infer an answer, merge two people into one response or count one person twice. Report eligible, partial, failed and ineligible results separately. A material misleading-claim concern blocks publication until addressed and retested.

No external action occurs under this document.
