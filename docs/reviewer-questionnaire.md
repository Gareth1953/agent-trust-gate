# Controlled Enterprise Reviewer Questionnaire

**Status:** EMPTY LOCAL QUESTIONNAIRE — NO RESPONSE HAS BEEN REQUESTED OR COLLECTED.

Complete after reviewing the controlled materials and before receiving corrective prompts. Approximately 15–20 minutes is proposed for the full review. Candid disagreement is useful; praise is not required.

## A. Comprehension

1. In one sentence, what does ATG do?
2. What consequential business action is being demonstrated?
3. What does the GatePass authorise?
4. What does it explicitly not authorise?
5. What is the difference between decision evidence and execution evidence?
6. Does ATG determine whether the bank details are correct?
7. Does ATG independently establish legal authority?

## B. Buyer and value

8. Who would own this problem in an organisation?
9. What existing controls might already address it?
10. Where could a residual control gap remain?
11. Would exact-action evidence add material value? Why or why not?
12. Is the proposed assessment understandable? What, if anything, is unclear?
13. The proposed range is **£5,000–£10,000 plus VAT where applicable — ESTIMATED AND NOT YET MARKET-VALIDATED.** Is it credible, too high, too low or impossible to judge? Why?

## C. Commercial signal

14. Would your organisation or a relevant client consider a scoped assessment?
15. What evidence would be required before paying?
16. What would prevent procurement?
17. Which second workflow would be the most credible expansion test?

The proposed next step is: **“Request a scoped workflow-governance assessment.”** This questionnaire is not an order, offer acceptance or commercial commitment.

## D. Claims and objections

18. Which wording appears overstated or unclear?
19. Which limitation is missing?
20. Does the material imply production readiness, fraud prevention, certification or legal authority?
21. Is ATG complementary to existing systems or redundant? Explain which controls make you reach that view.

Do not include credentials, bank details, personal data, supplier records, customer information or confidential architecture in an answer. The reviewer may remain unnamed in internal notes.
