**Primary call to action:** “Request a scoped workflow-governance assessment.”

## Required boundary recall

A reviewer passes the individual comprehension check only if they can correctly explain all three without prompting:

1. **Product:** ATG tests configured authority and evidence for one exact proposed action and issues a GatePass or refusal.
2. **Boundary:** The current material is local, synthetic and non-production; it does not connect to or act on identity, ERP, bank or payment systems and makes no outcome guarantee.
3. **Next step:** Request a scoped workflow-governance assessment using only a fictional or sanitised outline.

The reviewer should also distinguish identity verification, authority verification, business-policy evaluation, GatePass decision and separate execution evidence. Cryptographic evidence in the fixture supports integrity and binding, not truth, legality, fraud absence, bank-detail correctness, compliance or general agent safety.

## Pack pass rule

**At least four of five relevant reviewers must correctly explain the product, boundary and next step after one review.**

Record results only after separate authority permits controlled review. P3-M156A contacts no external reviewer, collects no response and makes no claim that this pass rule has been achieved.
