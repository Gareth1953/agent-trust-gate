# Enterprise Positioning Publication Gate

**Current decision:** `BLOCKED — LOCAL VALIDATION PACK ONLY`<br>
**Mission authority:** Gareth Price<br>
**Effect:** No publication, push, outreach, reviewer contact, buyer contact or homepage change is authorised.

## Conditions required before any positioning publication

Every condition below must be evidenced and Gareth must make an explicit, separate release decision:

- [ ] Gareth reviews every document, JSON fixture, source file, test, validator result and local demonstrator.
- [ ] All claims remain demonstrably accurate against the repository at the time of proposed publication.
- [ ] The fictional scenario has been rechecked to contain no real company, person, supplier, customer, employee, bank, account, contact, credential or secret data.
- [ ] All existing and new tests pass from a clean local checkout.
- [ ] Build and strict typecheck pass.
- [ ] Existing launch, claims, Agent Standing, Human Authority, exact-action, replay and local site-link validation pass.
- [ ] The enterprise-pack validator passes.
- [ ] Pricing remains visibly labelled **ESTIMATED AND NOT YET MARKET-VALIDATED.**
- [ ] No wording implies a production capability, production assurance or live integration.
- [ ] No wording claims prevention or absence of fraud.
- [ ] No wording claims regulatory, compliance, security or agent certification.
- [ ] No wording claims customer adoption, customer results or external reviewer endorsement.
- [ ] No wording claims business-wide deployment or proven horizontal reuse.
- [ ] The five stages remain distinct: identity verification, authority verification, business-policy evaluation, GatePass decision and separate execution evidence.
- [ ] Cryptographic statements are limited to integrity, binding and signer-related fixture evidence.
- [ ] The demonstrator remains static, local, synthetic and disconnected, with `externalActionPerformed: false`.
- [ ] A publication inventory names the exact assets and destination proposed for release.
- [ ] A privacy and data-handling check confirms that no real or sensitive input is requested.
- [ ] No external contact occurs without separate written approval from Gareth.
- [ ] The main public homepage is not replaced or modified without separate mission authority.

## Mandatory pre-release decisions for Gareth

Gareth must explicitly decide:

1. whether the positioning, buyer coalition and supplier-change wedge are approved;
2. whether the assessment name, 2–4 week estimate, estimated price range and exclusions are approved for controlled testing;
3. whether the exact claims and limitations are accurate enough for the intended audience;
4. whether the local demonstrator is approved for any controlled sharing;
5. which named assets, if any, may leave the local repository;
6. whether a defined group of reviewers may be contacted, by whom, through which channel and with what message;
7. whether sanitised feedback may be recorded and under what data-retention rules;
8. whether results meet the four-of-five reviewer pass rule;
9. whether a second-workflow reuse test should be designed under a separate mission;
10. whether any later publication, repository push, public-route change or buyer outreach receives separate mission authority.
